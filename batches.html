Batches & fees

How the classes run, and what they cost.

Everything is live and taught by me — no recorded lectures, no assistant instructors. Pick the format that suits the learner, then check which weekly slots are still open.

Three ways to learn

All three cover the same syllabus. The difference is pace, attention and price.

One-to-one mentoring

Just you and me. Best when the learner is very young, very advanced, or working to a deadline.

  • Timings set around your schedule
  • Pace adjusts session by session
  • Doubts cleared the moment they come up
  • Progress notes shared with parents
Get in touch for further details
Most popular

Small batch, maximum 6

A group small enough that everyone gets asked a question every session. Grouped by age, not just by course.

  • Fixed weekly slot, twice a week
  • Peers at a similar level and age
  • Shared projects and code reviews
  • Recording of each session's notes
Get in touch for further details

Weekend workshop

A short, self-contained course over two or three weekends. Popular with working professionals and homemakers.

  • Saturday or Sunday mornings
  • Complete topic, start to finish
  • Nothing assumed — beginners welcome
  • Worksheets to keep and revisit

Good to know

Before you enrol

First session is free Come and see how it goes. Enrol only if the learner is comfortable and you're happy with the fit.
Monthly, not annual Fees are paid a month at a time. No long lock-in, no joining fee.
Missed a class? Tell me a day ahead and the session is made up in the same month, subject to a free slot.
Siblings and referrals A concession applies when two learners from the same family join together. Ask when enquiring.

Ready to pick a slot?

Send me the learner's age and the course you have in mind, and I'll confirm availability and the current fee.
